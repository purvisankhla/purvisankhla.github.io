VAT rate for works of art

Works of art are only subject to the 9% rate if:

  • import from a non-EU country
  • the delivery by the artist who designed and created the work of art or the delivery by his heir
  • the supply by an entrepreneur who is not a reseller and who fully deducts the VAT on his purchase

An artist is someone who designs works such as paintings and sculptures that are considered works of art by society. If an artist sells a work of art through a legal entity (e.g., a private limited company), this is also subject to the 9% rate. However, the artwork must be entirely created by the artist and be recognizable as such.

Art objects are:

  • paintings, collages, decorative plates and drawings made by the artist
  • Original engravings, etchings, woodcuts and lithographs in limited editions.
    These must be made by hand and any technique may be used except mechanical and photomechanical reproduction techniques.
  • Screen prints and Giclée art prints
    must be handmade by the artist and hand-numbered and signed. The maximum edition is 250 copies. If the edition exceeds 250 copies, the entire edition is subject to the 21% tariff.
  • original statues and sculptures
  • tapestries and wall textiles made by hand according to the artist's design, up to a maximum edition of 8 pieces
  • unique ceramic objects that the artist has made and signed himself
  • enamel work on copper that the artist has made by hand, numbered and signed up to a maximum edition of 8 pieces
  • photographs taken by the artist and printed, numbered and signed by him or under his supervision, in a maximum edition of 30 prints.
    This maximum applies to all formats and media together.

The following are subject to the 21% rate:

  • construction drawings, technical drawings, topographical drawings and the like
  • hand-decorated objects
  • painted sets for theaters and studios
  • utensils
  • serially produced ceramics
  • works of art made by third parties on behalf of an artist

Rental of works of art

The rental of artworks by the artist themselves falls under the 9% rate. Lending or renting out artworks by others, such as art lending institutions, falls under the 21% rate.
